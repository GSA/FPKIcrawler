# INSTRUCTIONS & FORMATTING RULES:
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Add ONE thumbprint per line, please comment what certificate is being excluded (issuer and subject) in a comment above the thumbprint.
# 2. Both SHA-1 (40 hex characters) and SHA-256 (64 hex characters) are supported.
# 3. Formatting is flexible: colons (:) or spaces between hex pairs are ignored,
#    and inputs are case-insensitive (uppercase or lowercase).
# 4. Lines starting with '#' are treated as comments and ignored.
# 5. Empty lines or whitespace-only lines are ignored.
# 6. You can temporarily disable an exclusion by putting '#' in front of it.
# ==============================================================================
